# Recommended book outline

## 1. Overview
What the repo became.

## 2. How to read this book
Explain evidence, chunking, interpretation boundaries, and the recommended reading path.

## 3. Signals
Pressure profile, hotspot areas, commit-type mix.

## 4. Timeline
4-8 major phases.

## 5. Lessons at a glance
Card-sized summaries of what the team seems to have learned. Good for skimming.

## 5b. Deep lessons
Full-chapter treatments of the load-bearing lessons. Each has a one-liner, problem, what broke, what they settled on, transferable insight, evidence. See `deep-lesson-schema.md`.

## 6. Subsystem arcs
How coordinator, provider, UI, infra, packaging, and billing changed role.

## 7. Pivots
Before/after sections for major changes in product, architecture, or business model.

## 8. Pressure loops
Repeated problem clusters: packaging, runtime integrity, billing, rollback loops, etc.

## 9. Daily / chunk chapters
Chronological coverage of all active work.

## 10. Appendix
All commits, PRs, tags, key files, and evidence tables.
